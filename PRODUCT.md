# Lifeline

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform
web

## Stack
User selected code-first, static HTML/CSS/JavaScript. Vercel preview deployment requested.

## Users
Donors selecting a blood center, and hospital administrators finding sample donor profiles.

## Capabilities and Constraints
Two entry choices only. Donor center cards with bottom Apply buttons; request sent then approved after five seconds; placeholder profile and congratulatory popup. Hospital form with six details; filter dummy records; five suitable profiles at different centers; profile details and bottom Send Request with confirmation popup. No blood-center portal, email, phone numbers, OTP, or gradients. All approvals, availability, profiles and requests are simulated. No external messages or real medical processing.

## Brand Commitments
Restrained red and white visual direction selected with the code-first option. Lifeline is a provisional demo name.
